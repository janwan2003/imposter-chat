Privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

Oddclue is a free party word game. It works without accounts, and we keep as little as the game needs.

What we store

  • For each game: its name, settings, the words dealt, the clues and votes, and the imposter's guess.
  • For each player: the nickname you type and a random secret code that lets your phone rejoin as you. We store only a hash of that code.
  • On your device: that secret code, in your browser's local storage, and your last nickname so you don't have to retype it.
  • For our own statistics: how many games, joins and rounds happen each day, as plain totals. They say nothing about who played.
  • For abuse protection: a counter of requests per IP address for one hour. It is not linked to your nickname and is deleted after the hour.

What we don't do

No email, phone number or contacts are asked for. There are no ads, no tracking cookies and no third-party analytics. We never sell or share game data.

How long it is kept

A game and everything in it are deleted automatically 30 days after its last activity.

Where it is kept

The game runs on Cloudflare Workers, with game data in Cloudflare Durable Objects storage.

Contact

Questions or a request to delete a game sooner: open an issue on GitHub.
