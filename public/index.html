How to play

1

Share the link

Create a game and send the link to your group. You need 3 or more players.

2

Peek at your card

Everyone sees the secret word, except the imposter, who only sees the category.

3

Give one clue

Write a word that proves you know it, without giving it away. Clues stay hidden until all are in.

4

Vote out the faker

Read the clues and vote. If caught, the imposter can still steal points by guessing the word.

Questions

Do we all need to be online at the same time?

No. That's the point. Each person opens the link when they have a moment. The clues are revealed together once everyone has written one, and the page updates by itself if you're watching.

Can we play in the same room?

Yes. Everyone uses their own phone, so nobody passes a phone around or peeks at someone else's card.

How does scoring work?

Crew members score 1 point for voting for the imposter. The imposter scores 2 for getting away with it, and 2 more for guessing the secret word. Scores add up over rounds.

Do I need an account?

No. You pick a nickname, and your phone remembers you for this game. We don't ask for an email or phone number. See the privacy policy.
