The imposter word game, right in your group chat.

Everyone gets the same secret word, except one imposter who has to bluff. Each player gives a one-word clue on their own phone, whenever they like. Then the group votes out the faker.

Start a game

How to play

1

Share the link

Create a game and send the link to your group. You need 3 or more players.

2

Peek at your card

Everyone sees the secret word, except the imposter, who only sees the category.

3

Give one clue

Write a word that proves you know it, without giving it away. Clues stay hidden until all are in.

4

Vote out the faker

Read the clues and vote. If caught, the imposter can still steal points by guessing the word.
